How this screen works

Data. Weather is live from Open-Meteo. Itineraries and flight status are sample data standing in for the backend connection and the flight-status feed, timed from when you opened this page.

Time to act counts down to the next window that closes: boarding while the traveller is still at the origin, then landing. After landing it counts to leg 2's departure, which is recovery only. Flight feeds don't report boarding, so it's assumed to start 45 min before the estimated departure (KLM boards European flights 25–60 min before).

Projected slack = leg 2's estimated departure − leg 1's estimated arrival − the hub's minimum connection time (AMS: 50 min, a sample figure; 60 where we don't have one). An alert needs 2 checks in a row below 0. A cancellation is re-checked before anyone is alerted.

Matching. Each flight is matched to the Open-Meteo hour that covers its estimated departure (at its origin) or arrival (at its destination), then shown in airport local time. Weather never raises an alert here. It makes us check those flights more often, and it explains the alert.

Severe (starting thresholds): thunderstorm (WMO 95/96/99), freezing drizzle or rain (56/57/66/67), heavy snow (75/86), gusts ≥ 55 km/h, visibility under 1,000 m.
